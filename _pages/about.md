Thanks for reading, everyone. I've put together this site as an aggregator of my online presence, and also for a little blogging. If you're a colleague or recruiter, have a look. If we have similar technical interests, also have a look. My CV is clickable up at the top, and available in multiple forms.

I'm a lab scientist who has spent the last 17 years in infectious disease world; I'm extremely motivated by virology, molecular epidemiology, vaccine science, and all other related topics. I'm also a self-taught bioinformatician; some of my blog posts will involve taking things apart and putting them back together again. I don't really like black boxes.

The humane side of things is appealing; it's easy to forget that knowledge can affect people in unexpected ways, and that making an observation is not the same as having wisdom. So, some of my posts may deal with art, religion, or other things like that. Sometimes I might give my take on a recent piece of research.

Finally, please reach out if you like. I'm always looking for a good colleague. Maybe there's a collaboration in the works, or maybe you just think my writing could be better. Either way, link's at the left. Thanks!
